# FRIENDS of Sullivan's Island Elementary School — Website Background & Context
**Prepared for:** the team building the new FRIENDS website
**Prepared:** July 2026
**Purpose:** A single, detailed reference for everything a designer, copywriter, or developer needs to understand FRIENDS of SIES — who we are, the value we create, how we raise money, and how we're organized. The goal of the new site is to lead with **what we do and the value we provide**. The FRIENDS-vs-PTA distinction is included but intentionally treated as secondary "additional info," near the end.

---
## 1. The One-Liner
**FRIENDS of SIES is the funding engine behind what makes Sullivan's Island Elementary exceptional.** We are a parent-run organization that raises money to fund the teachers, programs, and resources the school district doesn't pay for — the things that turn a good public school into a remarkable one.

A tested internal framing to build the site's voice around:
> "FRIENDS funds the programs the district doesn't — the aquariums, the track, the interventionists, the art and music days."

Elevator pitch (from the donor materials, usable near-verbatim):
> "Our fundraising supports the extra programs and resources that make Sullivan's Island Elementary a special place to learn and grow."

**Vision / purpose (verbatim from the current site):**
> FRIENDS of SIES aims to "provide direct financial support to enhance the learning experience in ways that strengthen the academic, physical, social and emotional well-being of each student at SIES."

**Who we are (verbatim from the board page):**
> "FRIENDS of SIES is a parent-run board that raises funds to support the academic, physical, social and emotional well-being of each student at SIES."

**Formal mission (from governing documents):**
> To "raise funds to enhance the ability of Sullivan's Island Elementary School (SIES) to attain its educational goals."

The recurring four-part promise to weave throughout the site: **academic, physical, social, and emotional well-being** of every student.

## 3. What FRIENDS Does — The Value We Provide (THE CORE OF THE SITE)
This is the heart of the story. FRIENDS pays for the people, programs, and places that the Charleston County School District (CCSD) budget does not cover. When a family gives, this is what their money becomes:

**Teachers & instruction the district doesn't fund**
- Spanish instruction
- A certified technology teacher / technology education
- Additional teachers and academic interventionists (extra classroom support and intervention services for students who need it)
- Instructional resources and tools for educators
- Teacher and professional-development support

**Science, environment & hands-on learning**
- The wet lab and aquarium/touch tanks (live marine science on campus)
- The Green Heart gardening program (an outdoor, hands-on gardening curriculum run in the SIES garden)
- Science labs and materials

**Arts, music & enrichment**
- Art and music programs and enrichment days

**Physical space & student experience**
- Playground improvements
- Athletic facilities (e.g., the basketball court / track)

**Operations & student well-being**
- The "silent dismissal" initiative and tools like the School Dismissal Manager (making daily dismissal calmer and safer)
- Academic intervention and student support services

**How to frame it on the site:** organize these into a few clean value pillars — for example *Great Teachers*, *Hands-On Science & Nature*, *Arts & Enrichment*, *A Better Campus*, and *Every Student Supported*. Each pillar can carry one vivid, concrete example (the aquarium touch tanks, the Green Heart garden, the Spanish teacher) so the value feels tangible rather than abstract.

**A powerful, tested message line:**
> "Friends builds what's possible."

## 5. How We Raise Money — The Annual Rhythm
FRIENDS runs on four "moments" across the school year. The new site should give each a clear home (what it is, when it happens, how to participate) and make the year-round *Give to Grow* the always-on default.

**Directory & Signs — Fall (September)**
Local businesses sponsor placement in the school directory and on school-fence banner signage. Sponsorship puts a company's name in front of the whole school community and is fully tax-deductible. (Recent sponsors have included local realtors, builders, architects, and shops.)

**Soar on Skimmers Fun Run — Fall (next event: October 23, 2026, during the school day)**
An all-school event ("All students participate!") where kids collect pledges through personal, shareable fundraising links sent to family and friends. Registration runs through RunSignUp. This is the *participation and habit-building* fundraiser — the goal is to get as many kids actively involved as possible (a major growth target: from ~36 participating families up toward 150+).

**Give to Grow — Year-round annual campaign (peaks Nov–Dec)**
The core direct-ask campaign: "Everyone gets involved. Give whatever you like!" Goal: **100% school participation.** Families who donate receive SIES stickers, and the campaign leans on homeroom parents, the school newsletter, and social media. This is the site's primary, always-available "Donate" path.

**Spring Auction — Spring (March)**
FRIENDS' largest single fundraiser: an online silent auction (recently run on the Event.Gives platform) paired with in-person gatherings — a kickoff lunch and a cocktail party at local venues. The community donates experiences and items (art, travel, dining, wellness, "Principal for a Day," teacher experiences, the beloved "Paint the Rock" series), and local businesses sponsor. Ways to participate: **bid, donate an item, become a sponsor, or volunteer.**
- *Historical note on branding:* the auction has been known as **"Auction by the Sea"** (e.g., the 2023 "Wig & 'Stache Bash" at the Citadel Beach House, $100/ticket) and more recently themed around **"The Rock."** The new site should treat the auction as a durable annual event with an evolving theme each year.

**Always-on: Family & Corporate Donations**
Direct gifts from families and businesses any time of year, plus paid advertising in the school directory and on fence signage.

## 6. How Giving Works (Tax Status — important to get right)
FRIENDS raises and holds its money through the **Coastal Community Foundation of South Carolina (CCF)**, a 501(c)(3) public charity that acts as the tax-exempt custodian. In practice this means:
- **All donations to FRIENDS are tax-deductible** (processed through CCF's 501(c)(3) status).
- Funds flow through two CCF vehicles: the **Sullivan's Island School Endowment** (a permanent endowment) and the **Sullivan's Island School Grant Fund** (the working fund used for annual spending).
- The current public site states it plainly: *"FRIENDS of SIES is run through the Coastal Community Foundation, which is a 501(c)3… All donations are tax-deductible."*

**⚠️ One nuance to handle carefully in copy:** FRIENDS itself (legal name *Friends of Sullivan's Island Elementary School*, sometimes abbreviated **FOSIS**) is **not itself a separately registered 501(c)(3)** — it operates under CCF's umbrella. Some internal volunteer/donor materials have loosely described "FRIENDS is a 501(c)(3)," which is technically imprecise. The safe, accurate public phrasing is: *"Donations are tax-deductible through the Coastal Community Foundation, a 501(c)(3)."* Confirm exact wording with the treasurer/CCF before publishing.

## 9. History & Heritage (nice-to-have background)
FRIENDS grew out of a long island tradition of residents and local business leaders investing directly in their neighborhood school. Historically the effort was led by *"a printer, a restauranteer, a developer, a lawyer, a banker, among others"* — local professionals volunteering their expertise — who **created a permanent endowment at the Coastal Community Foundation** intended to be "their collective memorial" and to support the school "beyond what the Charleston County School District provides." The organization's spirit is explicitly intergenerational: modeling philanthropy for the kids who benefit from it. The annual auction ("Auction by the Sea") has been a signature island social event for years. This heritage is good material for an "About / Our Story" page but should support, not overshadow, the present-day value story.

## 11. Additional Info: FRIENDS vs. the PTA (keep secondary)
*Per the brief, this is background/"additional info," not a focus of the site — a short explainer page or FAQ entry, not a headline theme.*

SIES has **two** parent organizations that work together, and families often confuse them. The clean, friendly framing the board has landed on:
> **"PTA creates the experience. FRIENDS creates the impact."**
> Or: *"Two groups. One amazing school. Together, we make a difference."*

- **The PTA** brings the community and energy — teacher appreciation, community events, and the social fabric of the school.
- **FRIENDS** provides the funding and lasting impact — paying for the teachers, programs, science labs, aquarium, garden, arts, and student-support services that the district budget doesn't cover.

They complement each other (for example, at the Fun Run: *"PTA brings the energy and community; FRIENDS drives the fundraising and impact."*). The website should acknowledge this partnership warmly and briefly, then return the focus to FRIENDS' value.
